♻️ Reuse & Recycle

Give your unused items a second life. ReConnect helps route usable items towards social organizations and unsuitable items towards responsible reuse, recycling or upcycling.

Don't Throw It Away — ReConnect It

Many items that are no longer useful to one person can still be valuable to someone else. Through ReConnect, donors can submit information about their items and choose an appropriate next step.

🔄 How Reuse & Recycling Works

1

Submit Item

Tell us what item you want to donate, reuse or recycle.

2

Condition Check

Provide the condition of the item so the appropriate route can be considered.

3

Smart Routing

Usable items can be directed towards NGOs, while unsuitable items can be considered for recycling or upcycling.

4

Track the Journey

Keep the donation journey transparent through status updates.

🌱 Where Can Your Item Go?

🤝

Usable Item → NGO

If an item is usable and matches an NGO's requirements, it can be considered for donation to that organization.

♻️

Damaged / Unsuitable → Recycle

If an item is not suitable for direct donation, it can be considered for responsible recycling, reuse or upcycling through appropriate partners.
