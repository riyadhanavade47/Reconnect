🎓 College & NSS Connect

Connect your college, NSS unit or student club with NGOs and participate in meaningful social activities, donation drives, awareness programs and community service.

Make Student Participation More Meaningful

ReConnect helps colleges and NSS units connect with NGOs for organized community activities. Students can participate as a group and colleges can coordinate directly with NGOs.

🌍 Activities for Colleges & NSS

🏢

NGO Visit

Arrange a visit to an NGO to understand its work, meet the community and learn about social initiatives.

📦

Donation Drive

Organize collection drives for useful items such as clothes, books, food, stationery and other resources.

🤝

Group Volunteer Activity

Participate as a student group in community service activities coordinated with NGOs.

📚

Teaching & Awareness

Support educational programs, awareness campaigns and knowledge-sharing activities.

🌱

Environmental Activities

Participate in recycling, cleanliness drives, environmental awareness and sustainability activities.

💡

Awareness Campaign

Organize campaigns and educational activities related to important community and social issues.
